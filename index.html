  • Developing a statistical and computational framework for reconstructing heterogeneous multi-root cellular trajectories in the tumor microenvironment from single-cell and spatial transcriptomic data. 开发统计与计算框架，用于从单细胞与空间转录组数据中 重建肿瘤微环境的异质性多根细胞轨迹。
  • Designed a multi-stage methodology integrating root identification, multi-root tree construction, conditional mutual information, causal structure analysis, and local progression modelling to characterize distinct cellular developmental trajectories. 设计多阶段方法体系，整合根节点识别、 多根树构建、条件互信息、因果结构分析与 局部进程建模，以刻画不同的细胞发展轨迹。
  • Extended trajectory analysis through causal vector fields, global potential fields, and transfer entropy to quantify progression direction, convergence behaviour, and interactions between competing cellular trajectories. 通过因果向量场、全局势场和传递熵扩展轨迹分析， 以量化细胞进程方向、汇聚行为以及 竞争轨迹之间的相互作用。
  • Evaluated the framework across multiple single-cell and spatial transcriptomic datasets, including breast, lung, and melanoma Xenium datasets, using synthetic-data validation, benchmarking, sensitivity analysis, and bootstrap stability assessment. 在多个单细胞与空间转录组数据集上进行评估， 包括乳腺癌、肺癌与黑色素瘤 Xenium 数据， 并开展合成数据验证、基准比较、 敏感性分析和 Bootstrap 稳定性评估。

First Author | Manuscript submitted to Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine 第一作者｜ 论文已投稿至 Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine

Overview of the ADAPT framework: surveillance data preprocessing, positivity forecasting, analytical threshold derivation, Monte Carlo robustness analysis, policy evaluation, and diagnostic decision support. ADAPT 框架流程： 监测数据预处理、 阳性率预测、 解析阈值推导、 蒙特卡洛稳健性分析、 策略评估与诊断决策支持。

  • Developed a forecast-guided decision-support framework integrating time-series forecasting, statistical learning, cost-sensitive optimization, and resource-aware PCR triage. 构建预测引导型决策支持框架， 整合时间序列预测、 统计学习、 成本敏感优化与资源感知 PCR 分流策略。
  • Processed 78,068 dual-tested Brazilian SINAN dengue surveillance records from 2021–2025 and constructed a reproducible weekly positivity time series. 处理 2021–2025 年 78,068 条巴西 SINAN 登革热双检测监测记录， 并构建可复现的周度阳性率时间序列。
  • Implemented a bounded-output attention-guided LSTM in PyTorch, achieving held-out test MSE of 0.091, MAE of 0.227, and Pearson correlation of 0.719. 使用 PyTorch 实现 bounded-output attention-guided LSTM， 在测试集上取得 MSE 0.091、 MAE 0.227 和 Pearson 相关系数 0.719。
  • Derived a closed-form analytical decision threshold and used Monte Carlo simulations to evaluate diagnostic-policy robustness under stochastic sensitivity and specificity assumptions. 推导闭式解析决策阈值， 并使用蒙特卡洛模拟在随机灵敏度与 特异度假设下评估诊断策略稳健性。
  • Policy evaluation: in an 18-week held-out evaluation, ADAPT improved system PPV from 0.907 to 0.917 and reduced expected false positives from 12.99 to 11.43 relative to static NS1 screening. 策略评估： 在 18 周测试窗口中， ADAPT 将系统 PPV 从 0.907 提升至 0.917， 并相较静态 NS1 筛查将 期望假阳性数从 12.99 降至 11.43。
