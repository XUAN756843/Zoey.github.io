First Author | Manuscript submitted to Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine 第一作者｜ 论文已投稿至 Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine

ADAPT integrates surveillance-data processing, weekly positivity forecasting, analytical threshold derivation, Monte Carlo robustness analysis, and cost-sensitive diagnostic triage. ADAPT 整合监测数据处理、 周度阳性率预测、解析阈值推导、 蒙特卡洛稳健性分析 与成本敏感诊断分流。

  • Developed a forecast-guided decision-support framework integrating time-series forecasting, statistical learning, cost-sensitive optimization, and resource-aware PCR triage. 构建预测引导型决策支持框架， 整合时间序列预测、统计学习、 成本敏感优化与资源感知 PCR 分流。
  • Processed 78,068 dual-tested Brazilian SINAN dengue surveillance records from 2021–2025 and constructed a reproducible weekly positivity time series. 处理 2021–2025 年 78,068 条巴西 SINAN 登革热 双检测监测记录， 构建可复现的周度阳性率时间序列。
  • Implemented a bounded-output attention-guided LSTM in PyTorch, achieving held-out test MSE of 0.091, MAE of 0.227, and Pearson correlation of 0.719. 使用 PyTorch 实现 bounded-output attention-guided LSTM， 在测试集取得 MSE 0.091、 MAE 0.227 与 Pearson 相关系数 0.719。
  • Derived a closed-form analytical decision threshold and used Monte Carlo simulation to quantify policy robustness under uncertainty in diagnostic sensitivity and specificity. 推导闭式解析决策阈值， 并利用蒙特卡洛模拟 量化诊断灵敏度与特异度 不确定性下的策略稳健性。
  • Policy evaluation: in an 18-week held-out evaluation, ADAPT improved PPV from 0.907 to 0.917 and reduced expected false positives from 12.99 to 11.43 relative to static NS1 screening. 策略评估： 在 18 周测试窗口中， ADAPT 将 PPV 从 0.907 提升至 0.917， 并相较静态 NS1 筛查 将期望假阳性数 从 12.99 降至 11.43。
